---

🟣 Feed Page (Main Page Instructions)

1. General Functionality:

The Feed page is the main page of the app.

Everything visible on the Feed page must be clickable and functional — no dummy buttons.

Any feature that needs another system to work (e.g. comments, likes, or profiles) must have that feature built as part of this task.



---

🟣 Feed Page Features and Required Actions

2. Stories (New Feature — must be added):

Add a Stories bar at the top of the Feed (like Instagram or Facebook).

Each story should display a circular profile photo + username.

When a user taps a story:

It opens a full-screen story view.

Story view supports photos, videos, and text stories.

Users can swipe left/right to move between stories.

Add options to add story, delete story, mute, view viewers, and reply to stories.


Stories disappear after 24 hours automatically.

Backend must support storing and expiring stories.



---

3. Live Feature:

The “Go Live” button must be active.

When a user taps “Go Live,” it should:

Open camera + microphone access.

Allow user to add a title, choose who can view (public/followers/friends), and start live.

Viewers can comment, send likes, and follow during live.

Show live viewer count and end button.




---

4. Post Feature:

Clicking “Create Post” allows:

Adding photo, video, or text posts.

Optional location tag, caption, and hashtags.

Option to tag friends.


Posts appear in Feed instantly after upload.

Support like, comment, share, and save buttons for every post.

Each user’s name and photo in a post must link to their profile page.



---

5. Comments:

Tapping the comment icon opens a comment section below or in a popup.

Users can add, edit, delete, and reply to comments.

Show number of comments under the post.

Commenters’ names and photos are clickable (open profile).



---

6. Likes & Reactions:

Heart icon toggles between liked/unliked.

Show number of likes and list of users who liked when tapped.



---

7. Share:

Clicking share gives options:

Share to story

Send via message (Messenger feature)

Copy post link

Share externally (system share sheet)




---

8. Saved Posts:

Save icon should toggle and store posts in user’s “Saved” collection under their profile.



---

9. Feed Navigation:

Swipe down to refresh posts.

Infinite scroll for loading more posts.

Clicking on videos plays them inline, with mute/unmute, like TikTok/Instagram style.

Feed layout: vertical scroll, each post separated with space.



---

10. Integration and Dependencies:

All profile, post, and story clicks must lead to actual working pages.

If any feature (e.g. comments, likes, or lives) depends on another backend element, build that backend feature too.

No feature should be “for later” — everything connected should work.
