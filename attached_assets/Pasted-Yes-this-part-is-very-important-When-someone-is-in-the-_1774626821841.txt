---

SURNA Feed → Video Experience Spec

Core idea

When a user is scrolling the main feed and taps a video or enters the video area, the experience should feel:

smooth like Instagram/TikTok

connected to the feed

not like a totally separate app

immersive, but easy to exit back to feed


The user must always feel: “I am still inside SURNA feed, just deeper in the video layer.”


---

1) How users enter video mode from the feed

There should be 3 clear entry paths:

A. Tap a video post in the feed

If a video appears as a normal post card in the feed and the user taps it:

open that video in a full-screen vertical viewer

animate from the exact card position into full screen

do not hard cut unless performance forces it


B. Tap a dedicated video area/tab inside feed

If there is a “Videos” lane, button, or section:

open a vertical video stream

start from the first selected video

show related videos after it


C. Swipe up from a video preview card

Optional but premium:

if a feed card has a playable preview, user can tap or swipe up to expand into full video mode



---

2) Transition from feed to video viewer

This transition matters a lot.

Required behavior

When a user taps a video card:

the card should expand smoothly

corners gradually reduce as it becomes full-screen

surrounding feed fades away

video becomes active immediately


Timing

duration: 180ms–240ms

easing: soft ease-out

should feel quick, not theatrical


Important

Do not make it:

jarring

like opening a brand-new page

slow or dramatic


The user should feel: “I tapped the video and it naturally became the focus.”


---

3) What happens once inside the video layer

Once inside, user enters a vertical video stream.

Structure

selected video is first

next videos are stacked vertically

swipe up/down moves between videos

each video is full-screen or near full-screen


This should feel similar to modern short-form video browsing, but cleaner.


---

4) Relation between feed videos and video stream

This is very important for SURNA.

The video stream should not be random unless user intentionally opens a broad explore mode.

If user enters from feed:

Prioritize videos related to:

same interest cluster

same sport

same event

same team

same creator type

same place

same challenge/protest/topic


Example: If user tapped a football training clip, the next videos should likely be:

football highlights

team tryouts

coach tips

local events

related player stories


Not random beauty content or unrelated things.

This makes SURNA feel intelligent.


---

5) Top area of the video viewer

Keep it clean.

Top bar should include:

back/close button

optional small label like:

For You

Following

Team Videos

Event Clips


maybe subtle dropdown if you want category switching


Best layout

Top-left:

back arrow


Top-center or upper-left:

feed context label


Top-right:

optional overflow menu



---

6) Back behavior — this is critical

When user exits the video viewer:

they should return to the same place in the feed

same scroll position

same video card area

no refresh jump

no losing their position


This is non-negotiable.

If they tapped a video 12 posts down the feed, backing out should put them right there again.

That makes the app feel polished.


---

7) Feed card video preview behavior

Before entering full video mode, the feed card itself should behave nicely.

In-feed video card

can autoplay silently when visible

only one video autoplaying at a time

pause when mostly off-screen

show muted preview

tapping sound icon can unmute preview if needed


Preview duration

short looping preview is okay

but do not make the feed noisy or heavy


The feed card should tempt entry into video mode, not replace it.


---

8) Full-screen video UI inside feed-driven video mode

Keep it immersive but not overloaded.

Main areas:

creator/user/team info

caption

right-side actions

bottom interaction bar

contextual CTA buttons for SURNA objects



---

9) Right-side action stack

Use a vertical stack like modern video apps, but cleaner.

Show:

like

comment

share

save


Optional:

follow/join depending on object type


SURNA-specific behavior

If it is:

team video → show View Team / Join

event video → show View Event / Join

coach video → show View Coach / Book

challenge video → show Accept Challenge

petition/protest video → show Support / Sign / Learn More


So the video layer is not just entertainment. It becomes a gateway into the SURNA ecosystem.


---

10) Bottom area of video screen

The bottom should feel interactive and premium.

Include:

caption preview

creator/source name

optional location/sport/type tag

reply/comment field

quick reactions if it’s more story-like

plus button for deeper actions


If this video came from feed and is more public-post style:

emphasize comments, save, share


If it came from a story-like layer:

emphasize reply and reactions



---

11) Comments behavior from feed videos

When someone opens comments on a video from the feed:

comments should slide up as a bottom sheet

video stays visible behind

user should still feel in the same video context


Do not navigate away to a separate comment page unless necessary.

Comments should support:

replies

likes

reactions

@mentions

report

copy

translation if needed later



---

12) Scroll behavior inside video stream

Once user is in the video stream:

Swipe up/down

should snap naturally between videos

no half-broken positions

one strong video per screen


Preloading

preload next and previous video

instant playback on next video

avoid black loading flashes


Animation

smooth and tight

no sluggish transitions

the feed and video layer should feel like one connected ecosystem

loading video

playing

muted

buffering

saved

16) Micro-interactions that matter

These details will make it feel premium:

tap like → quick pop

open comments → sheet glides up smoothly

back button → returns exactly to feed position

pause on hold

subtle progress indicator if needed

caption expands smoothly

save/share feedback appears instantly

reaction toast like “Saved” or “Reaction sent” fades out softly

17) What not to do

Tell him not to do this:

no random jump from feed into unrelated videos

no losing feed position on back

no cluttered TikTok copy with too many icons

no hard page reload feeling

no separate disconnected video universe

no ugly lag when swiping to next video



---

18) Best product logic

This is the cleanest rule:

If user enters video from feed:

Start with that exact video, then continue with contextually related videos

If user enters from a dedicated Videos tab:

Show a broader discovery-style video stream

That keeps both experiences smart.
